## How to Read This Map

Each page has:
- **Primary keyword** — the one term this page must rank for. Used in H1, title tag, and URL (if not already correct).
- **Secondary keywords** — support terms woven into H2s, body copy, and image alt text.
- **Long-tail targets** — 3–5 word phrases used in product descriptions, FAQ schema, and meta descriptions.
- **Volume tier** — High (>10K/mo), Medium (1K–10K), Low (100–1K), Micro (<100)
- **Difficulty for DR 11** — Easy / Medium / Hard
