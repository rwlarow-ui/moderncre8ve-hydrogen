Bright Spots

High AOV ($1,965 per sale day)

When you do sell, you sell big. This is consistent with handcrafted furniture — fewer transactions but high-value. Your median sale-day is $2,121, meaning most orders cluster around the $2K+ mark.

Two strong peak seasons

Spring (Apr-May) and Fall (Sep-Oct) are your power months — together they account for 57% of annual revenue. This matches home furnishing buying patterns: spring refresh and pre-holiday nesting.

Q1 2026 recovery trend

After a brutal Dec-Jan slump (38-day zero-sale streak), revenue is climbing: $3K → $5.2K → $6.5K over Jan-Feb-Mar. Momentum is building heading into your strongest season.

Wednesday + Saturday are power days

Wednesday ($417/day avg) and Saturday ($328/day avg) generate the most revenue. Mid-week could be email/social campaign day; Saturday is natural browse-and-buy behavior.

Areas of Concern

86.6% zero-sale days

Only 46 of 366 days had any revenue at all. This is the single biggest issue — the store isn't converting on most days. Even for high-AOV furniture, this rate is exceptionally low. The problem isn't order size; it's order frequency.

July near-total collapse ($78)

July generated essentially no revenue — just two tiny transactions. This represents either a serious traffic problem, a seasonal dead zone, or a site/payment issue that went undetected. Worth investigating whether July traffic also dropped or if conversion specifically failed.

38-day zero-sale streak (Dec 12 – Jan 18)

Over five weeks with zero revenue spanning the holiday season. For a furniture brand, this is a missed opportunity — holiday gifting and New Year home refresh are peak intent periods for competitors.

H2 revenue declined 12.6%

The second half ($41.3K) underperformed the first half ($47.3K) despite including the holiday season. The Dec-Jan trough pulled down what should have been a strong period.

Friday is a dead zone ($55/day avg)

Friday generates 7x less revenue than Wednesday. If Friday traffic exists but doesn't convert, there may be a UX or urgency issue. If traffic also drops, it's a content/marketing gap.

Recommended Actions

Investigate July + Dec-Jan gaps

Cross-reference GA4 session data for these periods. If traffic was present but conversion dropped, the issue is on-site (payment, UX, broken flows). If traffic also disappeared, the issue is upstream (marketing, SEO, seasonal).

Launch mid-week email campaigns

Wednesday is your strongest day. Send your newsletter on Tuesday evenings to capitalize on Wednesday purchase intent. Test a "mid-week feature" series highlighting a single product with its process story.

Build a holiday campaign for Q4 2026

Last holiday season was a near-total miss. Plan a "Gift of Heirloom" campaign starting October — gift cards, expedited wax products (3-5 day ship), and "order now for spring delivery" messaging for furniture.

Increase sale-day frequency to 3x/week minimum

Your AOV is strong — you don't need bigger orders, you need more of them. Target 12+ sale days/month (vs current 3-6) through consistent traffic generation: blog content, email, social, and paid retargeting.

Activate the SEO content plan from the audit

Your dormant blog is directly contributing to low organic traffic. The SEO audit identified 20 keyword opportunities. Publishing 2-4 posts/month starting now will compound into your spring peak season.

Add a "quick ship" product line

Your wax products ship in 3-5 days. Feature them prominently as impulse/gift purchases to fill gaps between large furniture orders and reduce zero-sale days.
