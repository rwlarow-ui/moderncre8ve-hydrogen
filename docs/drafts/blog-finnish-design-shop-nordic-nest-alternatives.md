Looking for **Finnish Design Shop or Nordic Nest alternatives** for a solid wood dining table? Start with what matters most to you: a particular Nordic brand, a choice of hardwood and dimensions, or help getting the table into your home.

Finnish Design Shop and Nordic Nest both carry furniture, lighting, textiles and homeware from design brands. ModernCre8ve offers a different route: Scandinavian-inspired and mid-century modern dining tables handcrafted to order by Amish woodworkers in Ohio. Allow an estimated **12–16 weeks for production and delivery**; custom orders may require additional time.

## When each store is the right choice

**Finnish Design Shop** is a Finland-based retailer for Nordic design brands, furniture, lighting and accessories. It is a useful starting point when you have a specific designer or collection in mind. Its [US orders FAQ](https://www.finnishdesignshop.com/en-us/customer-service/orders) says duties and import costs are covered, while sales tax may apply at checkout. Some items ship through US partners, so an order does not necessarily travel from Finland. Check the item's availability and delivery options before ordering.

**Nordic Nest** is a Swedish retailer offering Scandinavian furniture alongside tableware, lighting, rugs and decor. Its furniture selection includes full-size dining tables, including extendable designs. For US buyers, its [shipping information](https://www.nordicnest.com/customer-service/shipping-information/) says tariffs, import fees and local sales taxes are not collected by Nordic Nest and are payable separately to the carrier or customs authority, typically around delivery. Review that policy alongside the shipping estimate rather than treating the checkout amount as the final delivered cost.

**ModernCre8ve** is worth considering when you want a solid American hardwood table made to order in Ohio. The three designs below offer natural cherry, hard maple, white oak and Eastern walnut, with custom dimensions available by quote. Choose by the shape you enjoy, the space you have and how often you need additional seating.
