## Finnish Design Shop and Nordic Nest alternatives for solid wood dining tables

**Short answer:** Finnish Design Shop and Nordic Nest are excellent for Nordic design brands, lighting, textiles and homeware, shipped from Europe. If you want a **solid hardwood dining table built to your size in the United States**, delivered and set up in your home, ModernCre8ve is the alternative. Our tables are handcrafted to order by Amish woodworkers in Ohio in 12–16 weeks, with white-glove delivery and assembly included.

### When each store is the right choice

**Shop Finnish Design Shop** for Finnish and Scandinavian design brands and classics, from glassware and textiles to lighting and furniture. It's a Finland-based online store that ships from its warehouse in Finland. Its US pages list duties and tariffs as included.

**Shop Nordic Nest** for Scandinavian homeware and decor: tableware, candles, vases and smaller furniture. It's a Swedish online store that ships to the US. Check the furniture shipping rate and any import fees at checkout.

**Choose ModernCre8ve** for the dining table itself, especially if you want:

- **Solid American hardwood:** natural walnut, white oak, cherry or hard maple, kiln-dried and FSC-certified. No veneer over particleboard.
- **A size that fits your room:** 64", 72" or 84" long, with up to three 18" leaves. A 108" table and custom dimensions are available by quote.
- **Delivery handled for you:** white-glove in-home delivery and assembly come with every table. There's no curbside drop-off and no flat-pack.
- **US-made:** each table is built to order in Ohio, not shipped across an ocean.

### Questions people ask

**Is there a US alternative to Finnish Design Shop for furniture?**
For dining tables, yes. ModernCre8ve builds solid hardwood mid-century and Scandinavian-style dining tables to order in Ohio and delivers them with white-glove setup. For Nordic brand accessories and lighting, Finnish Design Shop remains a great source.

**Which online store sells Scandinavian-style furniture with delivery and assembly?**
ModernCre8ve includes white-glove in-home delivery and assembly with every dining table. Many European stores ship furniture by freight or parcel, which usually means you assemble it yourself.

**Why does a handmade table take 12–16 weeks?**
Each table is built after you order it, in the wood, size and leaf count you choose. Nothing is pulled from a warehouse. That's also why a custom size is possible.

**Can I mix a ModernCre8ve table with Nordic chairs and decor?**
Yes. Our tables use the same natural hardwoods and clean lines found in Scandinavian design, so they pair well with Nordic chairs, lighting and textiles from either store.
