Key Finding: High Impressions, Very Low Click-Through

Your pages appeared 20,268 times in search results this week but only received 77 clicks (0.38% CTR). The industry average for furniture e-commerce is 1.5–3%. Your product pages specifically show just 0.11% CTR despite 8,004 impressions — indicating titles/descriptions need optimization to stand out in SERPs.

Brand Search is Your Strongest Channel

Brand queries ("modern cre8ve", "moderncre8ve") account for 34% of all clicks with 8.2% CTR — the only cluster performing above benchmarks. However, 62% of visitors are new, suggesting growing brand awareness. Non-brand discovery remains the challenge.

Product Pages Show Deep Engagement

Whale-Driven Revenue Model

Just 370 customers ($2K+ tier) account for $1.27M — 82% of all revenue. Your top 41 customers ($5K+) alone generated $275K. This is a classic high-AOV, relationship-driven business. Investing in post-purchase nurture, referrals, and white-glove service for these customers would yield outsized returns.

84% of "Customers" Have Never Purchased

3,838 of 4,555 records have zero orders — these are newsletter signups, form submissions, and abandoned carts. The "Churned Shopify signups" tag alone covers 2,597 people. A targeted re-engagement campaign or list cleanup could improve deliverability and reduce costs.

SMS is an Untapped Channel
