## 3. Target Audience

**Primary segment — The Intentional Home Buyer**

Adults 30–55 in US metro areas (Northeast, Pacific Coast, Upper Midwest) who are furnishing or renovating a home with a clear aesthetic vision. They've outgrown IKEA and reject fast furniture. They are researching for 3–8 weeks before purchasing, reading interior design content, and searching for specific styles (Japandi, mid-century, Scandinavian) rather than generic furniture terms.

Profile: "A design-conscious professional or couple who are struggling to find well-made modern furniture that doesn't look like everyone else's home. They're willing to wait 12–16 weeks for something handcrafted and are looking for a brand they can trust."

They discover via: Google search (style + product queries), Pinterest-driven searches, interior design blogs, and referrals from design professionals.

**Secondary segment — Interior designers and decorators** sourcing statement pieces for residential clients. They search by style, material, and lead time. Proof of craft quality and clear spec sheets matter to them.

**Buying stage focus for this campaign:** Consideration → Decision (people who already know what style they want and are actively comparing options or ready to buy).

## 5. Channel Strategy

### Channel 1: Product & Collection Pages (On-Page SEO)
**Why:** These are already indexed, have purchase intent, and improving them has the fastest payback window. 26 products × 16 collections = 42 high-value pages that can be optimized without creating new content.

**Tactics:**
- Rewrite title tags with keyword formula: `[Style] [Product Type] | Handcrafted | ModernCre8ve`
- Rewrite meta descriptions with benefit + CTA
- Add structured H1 → H2 → H3 hierarchy with keyword variants
- Add Product schema markup (name, price, availability, lead time note)
- Expand product descriptions to 300–500 words with semantic keyword coverage
- Optimize collection page copy with 150–250 word above-the-fold intro targeting category keywords
- Add internal links between related products and collections
- Compress and alt-tag all product images for image search

**Effort:** Medium | **Budget:** Low (owner-operated time or $200–400 for copywriting)

### Channel 2: Blog / Buyer's Guide Content
**Why:** High-intent informational queries (e.g., "best japandi coffee table," "japandi vs scandinavian furniture") drive consideration-stage traffic that converts when linked directly to product pages. At DR 11, moderncre8ve can realistically rank for long-tail informational keywords that larger competitors don't bother targeting.

**Tactics:**
- Publish 1 buyer's guide per month (3 total over 90 days)
- Each guide targets a specific style-product intersection with 1,200–2,000 words
- Every guide links to 3–5 specific product pages with contextual anchors
- Optimize guides for featured snippet format (numbered lists, comparison tables)

**Effort:** Medium | **Budget:** $300–600/month for 1–2 pieces per month if outsourced

### Channel 3: Technical SEO Foundation
**Why:** Hydrogen/Vite is a fast stack, but structured data, crawl efficiency, and Core Web Vitals need to be verified. Technical issues silently cap the ceiling of every other effort.

**Tactics:**
- Audit and fix crawl errors, redirect chains, and duplicate content (especially locale-prefixed routes)
- Implement breadcrumb schema on collection and product pages
- Verify and update sitemap.xml and robots.txt
- Add Organization and WebSite schema to root layout
- Confirm Core Web Vitals pass on mobile (especially LCP on product image carousels)
- Fix any missing canonical tags from Hydrogen's locale-aware routing

**Effort:** Low–Medium (one-time) | **Budget:** $200–500 one-time if outsourced

### Channel 4: Link Building (Earned)
**Why:** DR 11 with 229 referring domains is the primary ceiling on ranking potential. Modest, targeted link acquisition from relevant design/interiors sites can move DR meaningfully at this scale.

**Tactics:**
- Identify 20–30 interior design blogs, home décor publications, and Japandi/Scandinavian lifestyle sites for outreach
- Pitch guest posts or product features centered on "Japandi living room" and "handcrafted furniture" editorial angles
- Submit to curated design directories (Houzz, Chairish editorial, design roundups)
- Reclaim any unlinked brand mentions (use Ahrefs Alerts for brand mentions)

**Effort:** Medium–High | **Budget:** $200–400/month for outreach or freelance link building

### Channel 5: Image & Pinterest SEO (Owned/Earned)
**Why:** Furniture is a visual category. Google Image Search and Pinterest drive significant discovery traffic for this audience. Minimal extra effort for meaningful reach.

**Tactics:**
- Ensure all product images have descriptive, keyword-rich alt text and filenames
- Create Pinterest boards organized by style (Japandi, Scandinavian, Mid-Century) and link back to product pages
- Optimize Pinterest board descriptions with searchable terms

**Effort:** Low | **Budget:** $0

## 10. Risks and Mitigations

**Risk 1: Google algorithm update disrupts rankings mid-sprint**
*Likelihood: Low-Medium | Impact: High*
Mitigation: Focus on E-E-A-T signals (expertise, experience, authoritativeness) in all content — emphasize the craft story, handmade process, and artisan backgrounds. Algorithm-resistant content provides genuine value to users. Monitor GSC weekly for sudden traffic drops.

**Risk 2: 90-day timeline is too short to see link-building results in rankings**
*Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Medium*
Mitigation: Prioritize on-page optimization and content (fastest-payback activities) in Weeks 1–6. Link building should be considered a momentum builder for Q3, not a short-term traffic driver. Set DR improvement as a 6-month secondary goal, not a 90-day primary goal.

**Risk 3: Product description expansion creates duplicate content across similar products**
*Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Low-Medium*
Mitigation: Each product description must lead with unique selling points specific to that item (dimensions, materials, finish options, use case). Use Biome-linted schema fields to ensure canonical tags are correctly set across Hydrogen's locale-aware routing, especially for `/en-us/` vs. root URLs.
