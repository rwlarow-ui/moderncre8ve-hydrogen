### 2026-12-13 — Winter is hard on wood

```
Winter is hard on wood. Forced-air heat dries out the air, and solid wood moves with it.

Three habits that help:
· Keep the table out of the direct line of a heat vent
· Wipe spills right away, and skip harsh cleaners
· Give the top a coat of paste wax every few months
```
