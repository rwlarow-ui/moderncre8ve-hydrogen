# Search keyword demand for ModernCre8ve product categories (US, as of Oct 2026)

Method note: figures come from third-party keyword-tool pages (RankHero, seodata.dev, MerchantWords) seen via search snippets only. NOT Google Keyword Planner exports; many are global, and RankHero mixes in Etsy marketplace data. Directional estimates only.

## Highest-demand head and mid-tail keywords
### Takeaway
Style head terms are large ("mid century modern" ~250K, "scandinavian" ~201K, "japandi" ~110K monthly) but mostly inspiration queries. Product-level style terms run hundreds to low tens of thousands; Japandi product terms are small (hundreds to ~10K).
### Cited Findings
- "mid century modern": ~246,000/mo, CPC $1.35, KD 52/100, 66% US — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/mid-century-modern)
- "mid mod furniture" 90,500/mo at $2.17 CPC; "mid modern furniture" 49,500 at $2.35; "mid century furniture" 9,900 at $2.46; "best mid century furniture" 390 at $4.56 — [seodata.dev](https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/best-mid-century-furniture)
- "midcentury chair" 9,900/mo (global) — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/midcentury-chair); "mid century rug" 6,600, CPC $1.16 — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/mid-century-rug)
- Generic "sideboard" ~368,000/mo, KD 26, CPC $0.97 (not style-specific, likely global) — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/sideboard)
- Generic "dining room table" ~110,000/mo, KD 48 — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/dining-room-table)
- MerchantWords (Amazon): "midcentury dining table set" ~20,000; a "mid century mod dining table" row of 32,093 was ambiguous, verify — [MerchantWords](https://preview.merchantwords.com/search/us/mod%20table)
- Amazon data Jul-Nov 2025: mid-century sideboards/buffets out-search and out-sell dining table sets — [Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/mid-century-millennial-trend) (aggregator, moderate reliability)
- "scandinavian" ~201,000/mo, steady Jun 2025-May 2026; "scandinavian decor" ~3,600; "scandinavian table" ~1,300 global; "scandinavian lamp" ~2,400 — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/scandinavian)
- "japandi" ~110,000/mo, CPC $0.94, KD 42; "japandi style" 49,500, KD 34; "japandi coffee table" 3,600 (~1,069 Etsy listings); "japandi lamp" 9,900, KD 22; "japandi table" 880; "japandi shelf" 720; "japandi decor" 1,900 — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/japandi), [coffee table](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/japandi-coffee-table), [table](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/japandi-table), [shelf](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/japandi-shelf)
### Inferences
- Style + product terms (e.g. "japandi coffee table", low competition) look most attainable for a small made-to-order brand; head terms are dominated by marketplaces and big retailers.
- Generic product terms dwarf style-qualified ones; ranking for "mid century modern sideboard"-type terms is the realistic route into that demand.
- By volume: mid-century > Scandinavian (inflated by non-furniture meaning) > Japandi.
### Gaps
- No US-only Google Keyword Planner numbers for mid-century dining table / sideboard / credenza / coffee table, "scandinavian furniture", or "japandi bed / dining table". Needs Ahrefs/Semrush/GKP pull.
- RankHero figures internally inconsistent in places and mostly global.

## Growth or decline
### Takeaway
Japandi grew sharply 2022-2024 and appears plateaued at a high level by 2026; mid-century and Scandinavian look flat. Evidence for handcrafted/solid wood growth is qualitative; nothing found for "made to order".
### Cited Findings
- Japandi top trending interior style on Google in 2023: +108% YoY, ~7,850 avg monthly searches in that analysis — [RentCafe](https://www.rentcafe.com/blog/daily-living/trendiest-interior-design-styles-2023/)
- Yelp: Japandi searches +108% in 2024 (Yelp data) — [Apartment Therapy](https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/yelp-2025-trend-forecast-37438049)
- Pinterest Fall 2024: "Japanese living room design" +135% — [HFBusiness](https://hfbusiness.com/hfbnow/ArticleId/28102/study-shows-consumers-drawn-to-japandi-design-with-tranquility-simplicity)
- RankHero "japandi" flat ~110K over trailing year, one dip to 90,500 Dec 2025 — [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/japandi)
- "mid century furniture" highest-interest mid-century term, peak early Jan 2026 — [Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/mid-century-millennial-trend)
- "brown furniture" 1.5K volume, +52% growth, tied to return to solid wood (Sept 2025) — [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/topic/brown-furniture)
- Pinterest Predicts 2026 home themes are bold/expressive (Neo Deco, Afrohemian, Fun Haus), not minimal; "pendant lamp" +40% — [Apartment Therapy](https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/pinterest-predicts-2026-37522611)
- Trade commentary cites renewed interest in craftsmanship and ~6% CAGR for North American luxury wood furniture (press-release research, low reliability) — [Furniture News](https://furniturenews.net/trends/furniture-trend-forecast-2026-27-elena-mellowes-oak-furnitureland), [HTF MI](https://www.htfmarketintelligence.com/press-release/north-america-luxury-wood-furniture-market)
### Inferences
- Japandi growth phase is over: stable demand, little tailwind, low collapse risk.
- Pinterest 2026 signals favor warmer, curvier looks; mid-century/Deco-adjacent curved forms may benefit (retailer forecast, not Pinterest primary).
- "Handcrafted" and "solid wood" work better as copy differentiators than volume targets until quantified.
### Gaps
- No Google Trends export for 2025-2026 for any term.
- No data for "made to order", "handcrafted furniture", "solid wood furniture" volume or trend.

## Intent split, CPC, difficulty
### Takeaway
No published furniture-specific intent split found. CPCs for style product terms roughly $1-$4.50, "best ..." terms priciest; difficulty moderate (22-55/100 on RankHero's scale).
### Cited Findings
- CPCs: mid century modern $1.35; mid mod furniture $2.17; mid modern furniture $2.35; mid century furniture $2.46; best mid century furniture $4.56; sideboard $0.97; japandi $0.94; mid century rug $1.16.
- Difficulty (RankHero, Etsy-influenced): mid century modern 52; japandi 42; japandi style 34; japandi lamp 22; japandi wall art 55; sideboard 26; dining room table 48.
- Embryo top-100 furniture keyword list by room, no per-term intent labels in excerpt — [Embryo](https://embryo.com/blog/top-100-keywords-for-furniture-brands/)
- Generic intent definitions — [Search Atlas](https://searchatlas.com/keyword-intent)
### Inferences (estimates, not sourced)
- Style head terms mostly informational/browse; product-noun + style terms commercial/transactional. Split is a guess.
- Higher CPC on "best ..." suggests advertisers value commercial-investigation traffic; 12-16 week lead times favor content selling craftsmanship and build-to-order value.
### Gaps
- No furniture intent-split study; would need own SERP classification.
- Wax products: no keyword data gathered.
- Houzz, Wayfair, Etsy trend reports not retrieved.
- Semrush/Ahrefs US KD and CPC unavailable (Ahrefs MCP unauthenticated).
