# SEO Audit: moderncre8ve.com
**Date:** March 19, 2026  
**Audit type:** Full site audit  
**Domain:** moderncre8ve.com (Shopify Hydrogen / Oxygen)

---

## Executive Summary

ModernCre8ve has a **solid structural foundation** — strong schema markup (Organization, FurnitureStore, Product, BreadcrumbList), keyword-rich collection page titles, and 232 customer reviews providing valuable UGC. However, the site is leaving significant organic traffic on the table due to three critical issues: a **completely dormant blog** (last post: March 2016 — ten years ago), **duplicate/near-duplicate content across collection pages**, and **missing or weak H1 tags** on key pages. The biggest single unlock is launching a content program targeting long-tail furniture keywords where competitors like Countryside Amish Furniture and DutchCrafters already rank.

**Overall assessment:** Strong product-level SEO, but content strategy and on-page fundamentals need serious attention.
